Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim Yaa Seen Wal Qur'aanil Hakeem Innaka laminal mursaleen 'Ala siratim mustaqeem Tanzeelal 'Azeezir Raheem Li tundhira qawmam ma undhira aabaa'uhum fahum ghaafiloon Laqad haqqal qawlu 'ala aktharihim fahum la yu'minoon Inna ja'alna fee a'naaqihim aghlaalan fahiya ilal adhqaani fahum muqmahoon Wa ja'alna mim bayni aydeehim saddan wa min khalfihim saddan fa aghshaynaahum fahum la yubsiroon Wa sawaa'un 'alayhim a andhartahum am lam tundhirhum la yu'minoon Innama tundhiru manittaba'adh dhikra wa khashiyar Rahmaana bil ghaybi fa bashshirhu bi maghfiratin wa ajrin kareem Inna nahnu nuhyil mawtaa wa naktubu ma qaddamoo wa aathaarahum; wa kulla shay'in ahsaynaahu fee imaamim mubeen
